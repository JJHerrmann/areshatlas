The first divergence as it came to be called was, according to the Xilibanal Elves around 15 million years ago. They called it the [[Great Awakening]] and consider it to be the birth of the race at the hands of the Archfey [[Cherburius]]. This is estimated and attested to through the lore of their people, as explained by the dramatic play from the High Elf [[Telotecan]] that illustrates the oral myth of an agent of the Fey, Puck, coming as their herald, and informing them that their very creation had been a crime against nature. As such they were owed recompense, and Puck taught them a great many things about utilizing the mana and energies they'd been exposed to through their connections with the Feywild.

Author's Note: The play is quite long. Often performed on ceremonial holidays and can last up to 5 nights of shows from beginning to end.
