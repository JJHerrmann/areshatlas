# Vardic

The Central Human language. This note currently carries only the writing system;
the naming framework has not been written.

## Writing

Humans took their hand from their halfling and gnome neighbours. Full account in [[One Root, Five Hands]].

- **[canon]** Latinized Thaana: Latin-leaning letterforms with fewer accents.
- **[proposed]** Humans promoted the small folk's vowel marks back into full vowel letters and write left to right. The precedent is real: the Greeks turned spare Phoenician consonant signs into vowels.
- **[proposed]** A few unmistakably Thaana-derived consonant shapes are kept, so Common does not read as English in a funny font.
- **[open]** The hand is shared by every human language, not only Vardic. It is recorded here because the other human notes are empty.
