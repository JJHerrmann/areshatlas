# Bocafalante

## Overview
The Goblin conlang — Hungarian-structured, Portuguese-vocabulary. Goblins who speak it call themselves **Gobifalantes**.

## Culture
The Gobifalantes are depicted as an East India Company-style seafaring trade culture, with their own trading company (a "Companhia Marítima Gobifalante") — likely the direct ancestor of the "Goblin Exploration Guild" referenced elsewhere in the setting (see [[Paktonyelv]]).

## Related conlangs
- **Kovács** — the Hobgoblin equivalent, same Hungarian structural base, Russian vocabulary instead of Portuguese, coined the same day.
- **Paktonyelv** — the shared trade language uniting Hobgoblins, Bugbears, Orcs, and Goblins, derived separately from Hungarian-filtered Spanish.
