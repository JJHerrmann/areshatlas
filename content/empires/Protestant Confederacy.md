# Protestant Confederacy

One of the four Post-Yawning theological empires — and structurally the odd one out. Where the Catholic and Orthodox blocs are described as **Empires** (a single hierarchy, church-as-state), the Protestant bloc formed as a **defensive confederacy** — Northern European Protestant states banding together for mutual protection rather than consolidating under one religious hierarchy. Protestantism's own lack of a single central authority (no equivalent to a Pope or Patriarch) makes an "Empire" framing a poor fit; "confederacy" implies something looser: allied, not unified.

**Core territory** (per the [[Thaer]] Empire Map): Britain, the Netherlands, Denmark-Norway, Prussia, Iceland.
