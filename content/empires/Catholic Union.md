# Catholic Union

One of the four Post-Yawning theological empires. After 1705, the Catholic Church transformed from a religious institution into a full political-military **Empire of constituent kingdoms** — bishops and cardinals sitting alongside kings as real power brokers, with the Church itself fielding standing armies.

**Core territory** (per the [[Thaer]] Empire Map): France, Spain, Portugal, Italy/the Papal States, the Polish-Lithuanian Commonwealth, and Royal Hungary.

The clearest concrete example of how this plays out: the **War-Papa** role described on [[Gift I]]'s page — a wartime supreme commander the Papal State activates in crisis. That a Paladin can have "Church-militant commander" as an actual career track only makes sense because the Catholic Church *is* a literal empire here, not just a religious authority.
