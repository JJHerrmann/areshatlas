In 1705, over some seventy seconds, portals opened across the whole of the known world and set down the peoples of a sister-Earth — elves, dwarves, goblins and the rest — whose own world had been unmade by a burst of hard light. That event is the **Yawning**, and this registry is dated from it: the present year, 1867, is **Post-Exile 162** (also Anno Portarum, the same count).

What followed was consolidation by confession. The churches became states. Five theological empires — the Catholic Union, the Orthodox Empire, the Protestant Confederacy, the Sunni Caliphate, and the Shia Bloc — hold the map between them, and the borders are still under argument. Every entry here is filed as a document of that ministry: a registry number, an originating bureau, a classification, and the record of its own revision.
