# Vanya Sorokin

**Concept:** Russian Goblin Rogue, part of the Aegis of Equilibrium's Junior Warden party alongside [[Gift I]]. Leans into Slavic trickster-folklore energy (the "clever fool" archetype, à la Ivanushka durachok) rather than a generic sneak-thief read — "Sorokin," from *soroka* (magpie), a bird associated in Russian folklore with thieving and chatter.

## Level 1 Ledger

**Attributes:** DEX 15 · INT 14 · CON 13 · WIS 12 · CHA 10 · STR 8
**Vitality:** 9 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Guard:** 14 (leather and quickness)

**Domovoi's Wink** — when an ally has a foe's attention, he can slip in past their guard for a sharp extra hit (1d6). Named for the Slavic household spirit known for mischief and misdirection.

**Quick Fox Trick** — a burst of speed as a free action: dash, disengage, or duck out of sight, once per turn. Fox is the classic trickster figure in Russian folklore (Lisa Patrikeevna).

**Trained hands** — proficient with lockpicks and similar tools, stealth, and reading a room.

No spells — a Rogue's Level 1 tricks are entirely mundane, which is rather the point.
