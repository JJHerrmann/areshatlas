# Bjørn Ottarsson

**Concept:** Scandinavian Bugbear Druid, part of the Aegis of Equilibrium's Junior Warden party alongside [[Gift I]]. Fills the party's healer/support role. Leans into pre-Christian Norse shamanic tradition (*seiðr*, communion with land-spirits) rather than a generic forest-hermit read — and yes, a bugbear literally named "Bear" (*bjørn*) practicing that tradition is on the nose by design.
