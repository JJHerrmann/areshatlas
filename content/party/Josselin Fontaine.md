# Josselin Fontaine

**Concept:** French Human Bard, part of the Aegis of Equilibrium's Junior Warden party alongside [[Gift I]]. Fills the party's face/support role — a near-literal fit, since "troubadour" (the medieval touring poet-musician the Bard class is modeled on) is itself a French/Occitan word and tradition.

## Level 1 Ledger

**Attributes:** CHA 15 · DEX 14 · CON 13 · WIS 12 · INT 10 · STR 8
**Vitality:** 9 &nbsp;|&nbsp; **Guard:** 13 (leather, light on his feet)

**Troubadour's Verse** — a few times a day, he can offer an ally a bonus die to add to a roll they're about to make — a line of verse or a turn of phrase, timed right. His **Chanson-craft** spellcasting draws on the same tradition.

Cantrips known:
- **Wit's Edge** — a precisely-aimed insult that actually hurts, and rattles its target.

1st-level spells prepared:
- **Friendly Air** — makes a person warmly, temporarily well-disposed toward him.
- **Healing Refrain** — a sung or spoken word of encouragement that closes a wound at range.
