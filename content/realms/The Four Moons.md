Areshnaat keeps four moons. Three of them — **Red**, **Green** and **Blue** —
ride close and fast, bound to one another in a lock that none of them can leave.
**White**, the Pale moon, rides far out alone, and it is White that the months
are counted by.

| Moon | Distance | Period | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Red** | 61,500 km | 1.75 days | innermost, swiftest, burning |
| **Green** | 97,700 km | 3.5 days | the middle seat |
| **Blue** | 155,100 km | 7 days | outermost of the three, and the steadiest |
| **White** | 372,000 km | 28 days, full to full | the calendar moon |

## The lock

Red, Green and Blue keep a strict ratio: Red circles four times for Green's
two and Blue's one. The arrangement is not a coincidence and is not fragile —
it is the reason the three can run as close together as they do without
throwing one another out of the sky. Each pass tugs the others back into step.

It is also why they look the way they do.

**Red** pays for the lock. Held at the innermost seat and pulled at constantly
by the two above it, it is kneaded hard enough to stay molten, and it burns —
the swiftest of the four and the only one that is visibly on fire.

**Blue** is furthest from that punishment and keeps its ice. Its orbit is the
roundest and the least troubled of the three, which is why navigators and
astrologers who want a fixed thing to measure against measure against Blue.

**Green** sits between, worked over enough to have resurfaced but not enough
to burn, and shows the raw stone beneath.

## White, and the months

White is near enough a twin of the moon of Earth, a little closer in — some
twelve thousand kilometres, which is nothing to the eye. That small difference
is what makes the calendar work: White runs **full to full in twenty-eight
days**, so thirteen of its months make 364, and the year is thirteen months
and a single day over.

That leftover day belongs to no month.

White is also where [[Niwara]] is held, in [[The Binding of Niwara]] — and the
Pale moon is of the physical cosmos, not of [[Aamantlan]]. He was put somewhere
real and far, which is the whole point of the binding.

## Consequences

**The tides are strong, and they are exact.** Four moons pulling at once make
the water move hard — but because Red, Green and Blue are locked, their pull
does not wander. In seven days Red works three times, Green five and Blue six,
and then the whole pattern begins again precisely. Seven days is the tide-week,
four tide-weeks make White's month, and fifty-two make the year.

So the seas are violent and perfectly knowable at the same time, which is a
rarer thing than either. The seven days are counted as [[The Tide-Week]]. The seas off [[Ereslim]] are dangerously swift — fast
water, not unreadable water, and the difference is the whole of a pilot's
trade.

**The sun is swallowed more often.** White's disc runs a little larger than
Earth's moon against the same sun, so when it crosses, it covers — total
eclipses are the common kind here, and the ring of fire is the rarity.
