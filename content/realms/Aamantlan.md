**Aamantlan**, the Skyhome, is where the gods of Areshnaat keep. It is not a
place on any chart. Mortals do not go there, and the few reports of it are
inferences from the sky rather than accounts of arrival.

It has a light of its own. That light is the measure the pantheon is described
against: [[Niwara]] is called so bright that even the light of Aamantlan does
not shine through him, which is a statement about Niwara and, incidentally, the
plainest thing anyone has recorded about the Skyhome.

The gods are not confined to it. They keep there, but they come down, and when
they do they are met in [[Ereslim]] — far enough east that most mortals never
test the claim. Sailors' accounts place the gods' dwelling in Ereslim itself;
that is what the east coast believes, and it is incomplete rather than wrong.

## Names

Called **Šamaykoda** in Karakeel, which the dwarves use without conceding the
elvish account of who lives there.
