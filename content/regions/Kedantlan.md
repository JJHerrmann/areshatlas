# Kedantlan

Tags: **canon** = the author decided it, **proposed** = a suggestion awaiting a yes, **modelled** = output of the climate model, true of the map rather than decided as lore, **open** = undecided.

Region 23 of the survey. The starter region, and the ground the front-page survey map is drawn over. A Mediterranean-to-continental country of shield lakes and low relief, named in the elvish framework rather than the dwarven one.

- **[proposed]** Kedantlan is the ancient homeland of the elves: a chain of great lakes and winding river valleys running down to a warm inland sea, with a single land border to the rest of the world. If accepted, this settles the open question below about why an elvish-framework name sits here.

## The name

- **[canon]** Kedantlan is built in the Xibanal naming framework: a Hebrew triconsonantal root, a Nahuatl-style suffix, and optional Greek or Latin prestige affixes. See [[_Xibanal (Elvish)]].
- **[canon]** The root is *kdm*, carrying antiquity and what came before. The final *m* turns to *n* before a consonant, giving *kedan*. The suffix *-tlan* marks a land or region.
- **[canon]** The people are the **Kedanteca**. One person is a **Kedantecatl**.
- **[proposed]** Kedantlan reads as the older country the empire grew out of. The imperial homeland is already named **Xaltlan** in the codex, built the same way from the prestige root *Xal-* plus *-tlan*.
- **[open]** That Kedantlan carries an elvish-framework name is explained if the elf-homeland reading above is accepted, and otherwise is not. Rook decides.
- **[proposed]** Alternatives considered and set aside: **Yantlan**, sea-land, for the old seafloor; **Agantlan**, lake-land, for the great lake chain and the shield lakes around it.
- **[proposed]** Place names already coined in Xibanal for this ground: **Givatepec** (hill-place), **Xartlan** (forest land), **Etzcan** (settlement of trees), **Agancan** (lake town), **Kochavco** (star city or observatory), **Yarechco** (moon city), **Kedanco** (the ancient city).
- **[open]** These were built from the Xibanal entry alone. They have not been checked against the Wood Elf or Sea Elf rules, because [[_Ajnetik (Wood Elves)]] and [[_Ahvesil (Sea Elves)]] are both empty files. The check cannot be done until those notes exist.

## The land

- **[modelled]** Area about 4.1 million km². Mean height 256 m, highest point 1,104 m. Low, worn country rather than mountainous.
- **[modelled]** Old seafloor: the source gives this as 10% of the region in its summary figures and as about 18% in the rationale for the discarded name Yantlan. The two disagree and neither has been confirmed.
- **[proposed]** A chain of great lakes fills the north. The river valleys run down to a warm inland sea, and the region has a single land border to the rest of the world.
- **[modelled]** 489 thousand km² of lake surface across 309 lakes. Shield-lake country.
- **[modelled]** Largest river flow about 11,137 m³/s.
- **[modelled]** Ground cover on the survey map reads as wet forest, grass and steppe, dry ground and cool forest, with deep water paling toward the shore.

## Climate

- **[modelled]** Mean rainfall about 803 mm a year. Mean temperature about 12.8 °C.
- **[modelled]** Climate classes by area: Csa Mediterranean hot summer 47%, Dsa continental dry-summer hot 22%, Dfb continental humid warm 11%, Dsb continental dry-summer warm 10%, Csb Mediterranean warm summer 8%, Cwa dry-winter subtropical 2%.
- **[modelled]** The model gives wet winters, dry summers and mild winters.
- **[canon]** For play, treat the winter as hard and the summer as green.
- **[proposed]** Use the model's temperature curve for the *shape* of the seasons only, not their severity. Its real-Earth twin is the Quebec and Hudson Bay shield country, which behaves the opposite way: rain all year peaking in summer, winters well below freezing. The two disagree, and play follows the line above rather than either curve.

## Peoples

- **[canon]** Probably no Koda halls in the Kedantlan area, though related hill dwarf settlements are possible. See [[Dwarves and Duergar]].
- **[proposed]** The Mountainholms, the range holding the five Kords, sit about 2,300 km from Naharapan.
- **[open]** Are the Kedantlan hill dwarves linked far-flung septs, or severed ones? Carried over from the dwarven open questions, where it is unresolved.

## Mapping

- **[modelled]** The region is drawn on the tilted-Earth frame: real Earth relief rotated into the world's frame, then run through wind belts, ocean gyres, heat transport, rainfall and rain shadows.
- **[modelled]** The Kedantlan survey page states its frame as 18 N to 66 N, 12 W to 26 E.
- **[proposed]** The world is about one third Earth size. The survey page defaults to that scale, with travel at 24 miles a day on foot, 40 riding, and 72 for a longship on open water.
- **[modelled]** Worked sample point, for checking frame alignment: 36.1 N, 10.6 E in frame corresponds to 49.7 N, 74.7 W on real Earth, at 369 m elevation, climate Dsa, 533 mm/yr rainfall, 11.8 °C mean.
- **[open]** Whether the front-page header's frame still matches the reworked map. The header is registered to a top-left corner of 10 W, 52 N at 120 pixels per degree over 4440 by 3840 pixels, which works out to 10 W to 27 E and 20 N to 52 N. The survey page gives 12 W to 26 E and 18 N to 66 N. The two are different crops, which is survivable; what is not yet known is whether the underlying tilt changed, which would make the header's tiles and its Naharapan marker wrong rather than merely cropped.

## Open questions

- **[open]** Kedantlan's borders, neighbours and polities. Nothing in the source fixes them; only the regional envelope is modelled.
- **[open]** Whether the region's name predates or postdates Xaltlan in world history, which decides whether "the older country" is a geographic fact or an imperial story about itself.
