**Ereslim** lies far to the east: verdant forests, high clifftops, and
dangerously swift seas. It is a place in the world, not above it — but it is
where the gods come down to, and where they are met when they are anywhere in
reach of mortals at all.

They do not stay. The gods keep in [[Aamantlan]], the Skyhome, and Ereslim is
the ground they set foot on when they do not. That distinction is not one the
east coast makes.

## What mortals say of it

No mortal has done more than see such things in rumor and old ship's hand
tales, and none has returned to the civilized world to tell of it. The common
account is simply that the gods *live* in Ereslim. That is incomplete rather
than untrue: it is the only place gods are ever seen, so it is taken for the
whole of where they are.

## The divination temple

[[Gozara]] keeps a divination temple here, a massive pyramid that catches the
light of her husband [[Niwara]] and uses it as part of her predictions. It is
to this temple that [[Yatbara]] came to speak with her, fearing what she would
lose, in [[The Binding of Niwara]].
