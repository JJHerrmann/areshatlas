# Naharapan

*River temple.* Start city in [[Kedantlan]], built as a pyramid city, ancient ziggurat on the island.

## Name

- Listed as **Naharapan**, from nahar + pan.
- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Naharpan**, "along the river" (root n-h-r, suffix -pan).
- Why: Stray -a- between root and suffix; nothing in the name means temple.
- Status: provisional until ruled.
