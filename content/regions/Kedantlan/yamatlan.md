# Yamatlan

*Lake land.* Lake port in [[Kedantlan]], built as a ziggurat city.

## Name

- Listed as **Yamatlan**, from yam + tlan.
- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Aagantlan**, "lake land" (root a-g-m, suffix -tlan).
- Why: Yam means sea, not lake; a two-letter root needs the w filler (Yaamuhtlan if "sea land" is meant).
- Status: provisional until ruled.
