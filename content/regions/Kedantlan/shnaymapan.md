# Shnaymapan

*Two-waters temple.* River confluence in [[Kedantlan]], built as a ziggurat city.

## Name

- Listed as **Shnaymapan**, from shnayim + pan.
- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Xoonaymaayanco**, "two waters" (root sh-n-y + m-y-m, suffix -co).
- Why: sh becomes x; numbers are modifiers and take oo. Correct but long; a shorter coinage may suit the map better.
- Status: provisional until ruled.
