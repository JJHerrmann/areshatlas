[[Aavaraya]] is the Light Bringer, the Lord of Flames, and the Radiant Sun. He is intensity personified, so bright that even the light of [[Aamantlan]] (*Xibanal Elven, Karakeel: [[Aamantlan|Šamaykoda]]*) doesn't shine through him. [[Aavaraya]] decided one day that he was to inspect the denziens of Areshnaat, and chose to stand close, taking post by his wife, [[Giralaya]], to view the mortals. Travesty ensued. People suffered terrible burns, crops failed, and forests alighted. Infernos stretched the horizon from his brilliant visage. [[Baaraya]], angered at the destruction of his creation lashed at [[Aavaraya]] with his weapon, moving him away. [[Aavaraya]], annoyed by his brother shifted places, setting a new continent alight. [[Zimanaya]], the Time goddess, the knower of the ordained that was divined by [[Giralaya]], frowned. Mortals were ordained to exist. [[Aavaraya]], challenged by his brother, would not be willing to move.

[[Zimanaya]], considering, spoke to [[Baaraya]], suggesting to him a solution for the ails that [[Aavaraya]] wrought upon the populace. "Your brother merely wishes to watch the creations of your hands. He doesn't mean harm, but he cannot be permitted to be too close. I say that we entrap him in the Pale moon, so he may sit and watch those below, without causing more damages." [[Baaraya]], considered such a plan.

[[Baaraya]] feared the consequence of [[Giralaya]], losing her husband to the moon, and went to speak with her at her divination temple, a massive pyramid in [[Ereslim]] that caught the light of her husband and used it as part of her predictions.
