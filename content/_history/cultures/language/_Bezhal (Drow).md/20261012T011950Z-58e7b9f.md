# Bezhal

The drow tongue. Only the writing system is recorded so far; the naming
framework has not been written.

## Writing

The drow are the one people who write in a hand they did not inherit. Full
account in [[One Root, Five Hands]].

- **[canon]** The drow write elvish words in the **dwarven** hand, with adjustments for spelling.
- **[canon]** They are elf-lineage, so by descent they should carry the elf script. They do not. Every other people in Areshnaat uses the hand it inherited.
- **[open]** How and when the drow took up a dwarven hand. Nothing in the canon fixes it, and it is the most interesting unexplained thing in the writing material.
- **[open]** What the spelling adjustments are. The dwarven hand is a consonant baseline that demotes vowels to marks, while elvish writes vowels as full letters, so carrying elvish words in it has to solve that mismatch somehow.
- **[open]** Whether the result reads as a dialect of the dwarven hand or as a sixth hand in its own right.
