# Glossary

Quick lookup of recurring terms and names. Full development history for each is in [[Author/Areshnaat/00_Versioning_and_Provenance]].

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Areshnaat** (Areshatlas) | Current name for the setting; continuous lineage from Eoferan (2023) through Perimoria (2023-04 onward). Matches the `areshatlas` Vercel project. |
| **Eoferan** | The setting's earliest working name (Jan 2023) — same WUBRG pantheon structure as later Perimoria, but entirely different nation names. |
| **Perimoria** | The setting's second working name (Apr 2023 onward) — where the current continent, conlangs, and deity roster were locked in. |
| **Egunjain** | A region, not a world-name — the Dwarven Kingdoms' far-northern homeland. |
| **[[Orystria]]** | Supreme/detached god of the pantheon; unseen for generations, entombed at the center of the orbits of the five moons. |
| **The Five Mothers** | Alarisse (White), Luminara (Blue), Noctyra (Black), Ignissa (Red), Sylvanna (Green) — the mono-color goddesses beneath Orystria, each with a Right-Hand Son. |
| **[[Najmii Empire]]** | Arabic/Moorish/Latin-coded empire anchoring the setting's ~17-polity continent. |
| **[[Sil'K'uhlam Empire]]** | MesoLatin (Maya/Aztec/Roman)-coded empire in the Tezcalar region, druidic practices. |
| **[[Kasbahya]]** | Arabian/Turkish-coded halfling culture, underground kasbahs, home to a thieves'-guild. |
| **[[Dwarven Kingdoms]]** | Three realms — Emerald, Garnet, Onyx — in Egunjain, under a supreme "Emperor of Gems." |
| **Karakeel** | Per author clarification: the dwarven language by the Areshnaat era, not a kingdom name. |
| **[[Paktonyelv]]** | Trade language of the Goblin Exploration Guild; Hungarian-filtered Spanish. |
| **[[Bocafalante]]** | Goblin (Gobifalante) conlang; Hungarian structure, Portuguese vocabulary. |
| **Kovács** | Hobgoblin conlang; Hungarian structure, Russian vocabulary. |
| **Eladrin** | Former Fae-army soldiers, left behind in the material plane when the Fae were exiled to the Feywild after losing an ancient war against Giants and a "Dragonhorde." Conflicts with [[The Age of Poking Things]]'s later origin myth — unresolved, see that page. |
| **High Elves** | Japanese Edo-period-coded elf culture; source of the setting's four-moon calendar. Per [[The Age of Poking Things]] (2026), uplifted from Australopithecus-like proto-hominids by a reckless Summer Eladrin naturalist — the "Year of the Apology" scene. |
| **[[Maktaba Fahm Hikma]]** | "Library of Understanding/Wisdom" — the in-world scholarly institution behind the formal sapient-species taxonomy. |
| **The Four Moons** | White, Red, Green, Blue — White matching Earth's own moon exactly. |
