# Sil'K'uhlam Empire

## Overview
A MesoLatin (Maya/Aztec/Roman-coded) empire on the Tezcalar Peninsula, practicing druidic traditions. Earlier working name: the "MesoRoman" culture, confirmed as the same people once the rotated-real-Earth premise (equator N/S through Lisbon, Kamchatka pushed into the tropics) was locked in.

## Geography
Sits on a rotated real-world Earth — Kamchatka Peninsula's real-world location becomes tropical/equatorial under the setting's altered planetary orientation, giving the Sil'K'uhlam a lush rainforest/wetland/grassland environment rather than the real region's tundra.
