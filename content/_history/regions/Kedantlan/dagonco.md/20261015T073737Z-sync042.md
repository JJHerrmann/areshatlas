*Dagon's city.* Sea harbor in [[Kedantlan]], built as a Latin harbor.

## Name

- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Daaguhco**, "fish place" (root d-g, suffix -co).
- Why: Dagon is a raw god-name; the spec says the elves renamed those. Daaguhco (fish) is in dictionary v2. Option: keep Dagonco on purpose as a fossil of the Fae era.
