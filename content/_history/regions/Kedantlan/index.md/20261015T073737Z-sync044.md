## Region name

Kedantlan itself fails the rebuild: the root is q-d-m, not k-d-m, and dictionary v2 already lists **Qidantlan** ("ancient east") with the land suffix. Renaming the region is a ruling for Rook.

Real-Earth twins are in `_dm-notes.md`, kept out of the wikibox fields on purpose.
