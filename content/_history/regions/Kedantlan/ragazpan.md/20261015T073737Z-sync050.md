# Ragazpan

*Trembling water.* Falls town in [[Kedantlan]], built as a ziggurat city.

## Name

- Listed as **Ragazpan**, from ragaz + pan.
- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Raagazpan**, "along the trembling" (root r-g-z, suffix -pan).
- Why: Masculine needs long aa; nothing in the name means water.
- Status: provisional until ruled.
