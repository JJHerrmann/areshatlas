# Selapan

*Rock temple.* Cliff precinct in [[Kedantlan]], built as a rock-cut.

## Name

- Listed as **Selapan**, from sela + pan.
- Under the Xibanal rebuild this becomes **Saalatepec**, "rock heights" (root s-l-e, suffix -tepec).
- Why: -pan is for directions; cliff places take -tepec; sela is masculine (aa), final ayin silent.
- Status: provisional until ruled.
