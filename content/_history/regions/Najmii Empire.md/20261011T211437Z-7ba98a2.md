# Najmii Empire

## Overview
Arabic/Moorish/Latin-coded empire, anchoring a continent of roughly 17 named polities spanning nearly every conceivable government type: Empire, Free Territory, Confederacy, Kingdoms, Sultanate, Pentarchy, Divine Duchy, Despotate, Duchy, Ulus, Quinquevirate, League.

## Neighboring cultures
- **Severijänet** ("Northmen") — a Finno-Russian-coded culture sharing the continent
- Three distinct dwarven cultures with Turkish-derived endonyms: Mountain Dwarves (*Taşdağlı*), Hill Dwarves (*Kayaçiftçi*), Duergar (*Taş-İlk*) — see [[Dwarven Kingdoms]]
- At least one Dutch-coded culture (a "Divine Duchy," *Goddelijke Hertogdom*)

## Institutions
Home to **[[Maktaba Fahm Hikma]]** ("Library of Understanding/Wisdom"), the scholarly institution behind the setting's formal four-branch sapient-species taxonomy (Theriomorphic, Elderinae, Mythidae, Goblinoidea).
