Ooxapyotl is a prime primordial. Inactive as far as personification, but the wellmouth of magic in general in Areshnaat. If you have unexplained divine power, spells, paladin powers from concepts, etc, it's Ooxapyotl's power that flows through you. The god or patron that asks for nothing, but to live.
# Ooxapyotl

## Liturgical structure
Each deity in the pantheon is given, per the founding session: a portfolio, a holy day/cause of celebration, a description of how followers celebrate, and a death ritual for followers. Example (Alarisse): celebrated on the "Harmonious Solstice" (equal day/night length); followers process in white and gold, perform acts of service; the dead are wrapped in white linen and interred in a sacred crypt.

## The "true name" project (Zoquean root-language work)
A later session attempted to give Orystria a constructed "true name" — **Niksnaak Tonatiak** — built from a Zoquean-based proto-language intended as this world's Proto-Indo-European analog, the root ancestor of the setting's other conlangs (like Paktonyelv). Not yet reconciled against the rest of the pantheon's naming.
> **Source:** `Deity Naming Project Begins - 2024-06-15.md` · `2024-Q2`

## Naming note
"Narset," a three-color demigoddess named in the founding pantheon session, was explicitly flagged by the author in that same conversation as "(temporary name, lifted from Magic)" — confirmed placeholder, not an intentional connection to the "Narset" name used elsewhere (an early dropped Mythic Comics AI-companion name, and a real Magic-card reference in unrelated 2026 material).
