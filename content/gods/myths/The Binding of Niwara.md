Niwara is the Light Bringer, the Lord of Flames, and the Radiant Sun. He is intensity workpersonified, so bright that even the light of (Nyx) doesn't shine through him. Niwara decided one day that he was to inspect the denziens of Areshnaat, and chose to stand close, taking post by his wife, [[Gozara]], to view the mortals. Travesty ensued. People suffered terrible burns, crops failed, and forests alighted. Infernos stretched the horizon from his brilliant visage. [[Yatbara]], angered at the destruction of his creation lashed at Niwara with his weapon, moving him away. Niwara, annoyed by his brother shifted places, setting a new continent alight. [[Zemana]], the Time goddess, the knower of the ordained that was divined by Gozara, frowned. Mortals were ordained to exist. Niwara, challenged by his brother, would not be willing to move.

[[Zemana]], considering, spoke to [[Yatbara]], suggesting to him a solution for the ails that Niwara wrought upon the populace. "Your brother merely wishes to watch the creations of your hands. He doesn't mean harm, but he cannot be permitted to be too close. I say that we entrap him in the Pale moon, so he may sit and watch those below, without causing more damages." Yatbara, considered such a plan.

Yatbara feared the consequence of Gozara, losing her husband to the moon, and went to speak with her at her divintation temple, a massive pyramid in Ereslim that caught the light of her husband and used it as part of her predictions.

## Open questions

- Who is "(Nyx)"? The name matches two candidate gods and is not settled.
- Which brother challenges Niwara? The text names a brother but not which one;
  the lore log gives Niwara the brother Yatbara and the half-siblings Lotan and
  Leyala.
- Which continent is set alight, and is the burned land the setting's deserts?
  Rook's "I think the burns are our deserts" is a working read, not a ruling.
- The telling breaks off at Gozara's temple; the binding itself is not yet written.
